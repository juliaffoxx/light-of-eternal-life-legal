Privacy Policy

Light of Eternal Life · Effective November 15, 2026

Reading and preferences

The app keeps your reading position, saved stories, language and settings on your device. It fetches stories and artwork through Cloudflare. Standard service logs may include your IP address and request details to serve content and maintain the service.

Analytics

You can choose to share usage analytics in Settings. If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives basic app activity, a resettable installation identifier, device and diagnostic information, general location derived from IP addresses, and information about purchases such as the product and price. Our events cover the introduction, opening the subscription screen and purchase or restore outcomes. We keep the passages you read, prayers and saved stories out of those events. We restrict analytics to measuring the app and disable advertising features.

Subscriptions and support

Apple handles payments and may share transaction details needed to confirm access, restore purchases and support family sharing. We rely on Apple for payment details. If you email support, we use your email and the details you choose to share to respond.

Your choices and retention

You can turn off analytics in Settings. This stops further collection and resets the app's analytics identifier. Device settings remain until you clear the app’s data or remove the app. Service and support data are kept as needed to operate the app and resolve requests, subject to applicable obligations. Email us to ask about your data or request deletion.
